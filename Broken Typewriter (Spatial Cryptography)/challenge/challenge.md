# Broken Typewriter (Spatial Cryptography)

## Story
A help desk intern submitted a flag, but their keyboard hand position was slightly off while typing.
Every letter landed one key to the right on a standard QWERTY keyboard.

## Your Task
The text in `challenge.txt` is the mistyped flag.
Recover the original flag.

## Rules
- Use a normal QWERTY keyboard layout.
- Reverse the typing error by moving each letter one key to the left.
- Keep symbols like `{`, `}`, and `_` unchanged.
- Flag format is: `ZeroSecure_CTF{...}`

Good luck.

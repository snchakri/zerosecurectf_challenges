# The Mask's Bell — solution

1. Recover the seven climb flags in order: Prime Hour, Thirteenth Name, Bridge of Bells, Ash Gate, Hall of Wards, Chamber of Echoes, Keystone Seal.
2. Read the dossier and identify the final gate answer `victorcynthiareed`.
3. The brief says those seven flags followed by the three names determine the key for `sealed.bin`.

## Archive limitation

The sealed payload is preserved, but its key derivation, cipher mode, and serialization were not documented in the available author files. The registered answer is retained; a full independent decryption was not verified.

`accepted_flags.txt` is the event scoring record, not a substitute for cryptographic recovery.

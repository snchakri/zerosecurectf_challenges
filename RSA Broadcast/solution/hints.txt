ZeroSecure CTF 2024 — Broadcast: Hints
=======================================
(For challenge organizers / hint release system)

HINT 1  [cost: 10 pts]
----------------------
Notice the public exponent: e = 3.
The same message was sent to THREE different people.
What happens mathematically when you raise a number to the power 3
and reduce it mod three different values?

HINT 2  [cost: 20 pts]
----------------------
Look up "Håstad's Broadcast Attack".
When the same plaintext m is encrypted with e=3 to three recipients:

    c1 ≡ m³  (mod n1)
    c2 ≡ m³  (mod n2)
    c3 ≡ m³  (mod n3)

If the three moduli n1, n2, n3 are pairwise coprime (they are),
a famous theorem in number theory lets you solve this system of
congruences for a UNIQUE value modulo n1*n2*n3.

HINT 3  [cost: 30 pts]
----------------------
The theorem from Hint 2 is the Chinese Remainder Theorem (CRT).

CRT gives you x such that:
    x ≡ c1 (mod n1)
    x ≡ c2 (mod n2)
    x ≡ c3 (mod n3)

Here x = m³ over the integers (not reduced mod anything).
Because m is small enough that m³ < n1*n2*n3, you recover m³ exactly.
Now just take the integer cube root — no modular arithmetic needed.

Python: from sympy import integer_nthroot
        m, exact = integer_nthroot(x, 3)

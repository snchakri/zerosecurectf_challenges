Finally rebuilt my encrypted field-notes vault. Note to self so I stop forgetting: the vault key is

sha256( handle ":" place ":" author ":" serial )
  • handle = this account's handle (the one that actually took the photo — check the shot's EXIF Artist if an impersonator shows up).
  • place = the landmark in field_01.jpg, resolved against my offline gazetteer.json (use its slug).
  • author = the Author field baked into journal.pdf.
  • serial = the TLS cert serial for my own subdomain in ct_log.json (NOT the look-alike).

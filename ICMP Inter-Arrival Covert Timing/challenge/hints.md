# Staged Hint Ladder: ICMP Inter-Arrival Covert Timing

### Hint Level 1
Covert timing channels modulate the time delta between consecutive packets (delta-T).

### Hint Level 2
Threshold classification: intervals around 100ms represent binary '0', while intervals around 500ms represent binary '1'.

### Hint Level 3
Demodulate the binary bitstream and convert into 8-bit ASCII characters.

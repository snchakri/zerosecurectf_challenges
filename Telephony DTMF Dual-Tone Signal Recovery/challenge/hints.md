# Staged Hint Ladder: Telephony DTMF Dual-Tone Signal Recovery

### Hint Level 1
DTMF keypad signals combine two simultaneous sine waves: a low group (697-941 Hz) and a high group (1209-1477 Hz).

### Hint Level 2
Use the Goertzel algorithm or Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) peak detection to identify frequencies in each audio segment.

### Hint Level 3
Map detected frequency pairs to corresponding telephone digits.

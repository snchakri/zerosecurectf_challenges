PERSONAL ARCHIVE — M. HORAKOVA
recovered from storage unit, Brno, 2023

------------------------------------------------------------
notes re: WARD 7 documents (received from Novak, Nov 2003)
------------------------------------------------------------

Nov 14 2003
Novak called. Said he found something during the Svratka
decommission. Keystore file from the old relay system.
Two admin keys. Same document encrypted twice.
He said the guy who built it was sloppy. "Same inputs,
same machine, one afternoon. He probably used the same
seed." I don't know what that means.

Nov 17 2003
Tried to open the ciphertext files. No luck. They're just
long numbers. Novak said the keys are in the keystore and
that's all you need. "You don't need the private key if
you know what you're looking for." He wouldn't explain
further.

Nov 21 2003
Novak not answering. Went to his apartment. Neighbour said
he left suddenly. Left most of his things.

Nov 28 2003
I've been staring at these numbers for a week.
The two public exponents are different. The modulus is the
same. Is that normal? Is that supposed to happen?
I don't know enough to know if this is a mistake or
something else.

[undated -- much later, handwritten on a sticky note attached to the file]
CASE-1943-29
He said the case number was the only thing that survived
outside the system. I wrote it down in case it mattered.
I never found out what it referred to.
